# How to Share Files Between Android and PC Without Internet

Need to move photos, videos, or documents between your phone and computer, but you have no USB cable and no internet connection? Xender is a popular free app that solves exactly this problem. This guide walks you through everything a beginner needs to know, from phone-to-phone transfers to connecting your phone to a PC. For extra background and walkthroughs, see the [Xender offline file transfer guide](https://xendrs.com/).

## What Is Xender?

Xender is a file-sharing application for Android, iPhone, Windows, and Mac. Instead of using the internet, it creates a direct wireless connection between devices — usually through Wi-Fi Direct or a phone hotspot — so files travel straight from one device to another. Because no internet is involved, transfers are fast (often many times faster than Bluetooth) and use no mobile data. You can send photos, videos, music, documents, apps, and even entire folders. Both the sender and the receiver need the app installed on mobile devices; for PC transfers, you only need a web browser on the computer.

## Share Files Between Two Android Phones

1. Install Xender from the Play Store on both phones and open it.
2. On the sending phone, tap the **+** button or **Send**, then choose the photos, videos, or files you want to share.
3. On the receiving phone, tap **Receive** (or the **+** button, then Receive). The phone will display a QR code and a radar-style screen.
4. On the sender's screen, the receiver's avatar or device name will appear. Tap it to connect.
5. The transfer starts automatically. Keep both phones close together and leave the app open until the progress bar completes.

## Connect Your Phone to a PC

1. Make sure your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network, or turn on your phone's mobile hotspot and connect the PC to it. No actual internet is required.
2. Open Xender on your phone, tap your profile icon or the menu, and choose **Connect to PC** (on some versions this is called Connect PC or Web).
3. Choose Scan mode. On your computer, open a browser and go to the web address shown in the app.
4. Scan the QR code on the computer screen with your phone. Tap **Accept** or **Connect** on the phone when asked.
5. Your phone's files now appear in the PC browser. You can download files to the computer or drag and drop files from the computer to upload them to the phone.

## Share With a Group

Xender can share files with several people at once, which is handy for events or classrooms.

1. Open Xender and tap the **+** button, then select **Create Group**.
2. The group creator's phone becomes a hotspot. Other people open Xender, tap **Join Group**, and connect to the group via the QR code or by tapping the group name on the radar screen.
3. Once everyone has joined, anyone can send files and every member receives them.

## Tips for Smooth Transfers

- Keep devices within a few meters of each other during the transfer.
- Grant Xender the storage and location permissions it asks for — Android needs location access to scan for nearby devices over Wi-Fi.
- Keep the screen on and the app in the foreground; some phones pause transfers when the app is minimized.
- Update Xender on all devices so everyone uses a compatible version.
- For very large video files, transfer them in smaller batches if a transfer stalls.
- When you are finished with a PC session, tap **Disconnect** in the app to close the connection.
